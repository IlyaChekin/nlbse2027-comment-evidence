# Third-party sources

- Contest data and baseline materials are published by the NLBSE organizers. The data manifest gives exact dataset revisions and hashes. [Competition repository](https://github.com/nlbse2027/code-comment-classification) · [Dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NLBSE/nlbse27-code-comment-classification)
- WeakSATD rule files and comment preprocessing are from the MADE-WIC replication archive. Its CC BY 4.0 attribution and runner adaptations are documented in `nlbse/round19/weak_satd/RULE_SOURCE.md` in the model ZIP. [Original archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12567874) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- CodeT5+ and the Microsoft model assets used during training are documented in `nlbse/portfolio/upstream/SOURCES.md`. Model-specific license files and notices take precedence for these components. [Model references](nlbse/portfolio/upstream/SOURCES.md)
